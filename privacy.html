Privacy

Last updated September 2026

startuponx.com is a directory of startups. This page explains what we collect and why, in plain English.

What we collect

Your email address, when you sign in with Google. The startup details you submit: name, description, URL, category, and logo. Payment is verified via UPI transaction ID — we never see or store your bank details.

How we use it

To show your listing on the site, to let you sign back in and manage it, and to contact you about your submission or subscription.

Who we share it with

Firebase (Google) handles our authentication and database. We don't sell your data.

Your choices

Email us at startuponx@gmail.com to update or remove your listing and account data at any time.

Contact

Reach us through the contact form or at startuponx@gmail.com
