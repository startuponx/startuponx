Why listing your startup in a directory still works in 2026

September 2026·4 min read

People keep saying directories are dead. They're wrong. A good directory listing gives you three things that no paid ad can: a permanent backlink, a searchable public presence, and a community of buyers actively looking for tools.

1. SEO — a backlink that compounds over time

Every directory listing is a backlink from another domain to yours. Backlinks from established sites tell Google your site is trusted. Unlike an Instagram ad that stops the moment you stop paying, a directory listing keeps working for you every single day — for free, after the first payment.

For Indian SaaS founders specifically, being listed on an India-focused directory also helps with local SEO — searches like "best SaaS tools for Indian businesses" or "Indian startup for [category]."

2. AEO — answers for AI search engines

AI-powered search tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overview are changing how people find products. These tools pull from structured content on the web — including directories. When someone asks "what are the best Indian AI startups?" a directory like StartupOnX India is exactly the kind of source these tools cite.

Getting listed now means your startup has a chance of appearing in AI-generated answers before your competitors even think about it.

3. Community discovery

The people browsing a startup directory are not casual scrollers. They're founders, investors, early adopters, and product people actively looking for new tools. That's a different quality of visitor than someone who saw your ad while watching a reel.

How much does it cost?

On StartupOnX India, a listing costs ₹149/month. That's less than a cup of coffee in a Bangalore café. For a permanent backlink, potential AI citations, and community exposure — the ROI calculation is obvious.
