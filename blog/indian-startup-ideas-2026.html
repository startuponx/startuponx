50 Indian startup ideas for 2026 — problems worth solving in India

September 2026·10 min read

India has 600 million internet users, 60 million small businesses, 700 million farmers, and one of the youngest populations in the world. The startup ideas that work here don't need to copy Silicon Valley — they need to solve Indian problems at Indian prices.

Here are 50 real startup ideas for Indian founders in 2026, organised by sector. These are problems that still exist, still hurt people, and still have no good solution.

Fintech

  • UPI-based B2B invoice payments — most small businesses still pay suppliers in cash. A simple UPI-native invoicing tool could be huge.
  • Credit scoring for gig workers — Swiggy and Zomato delivery partners can't get loans. Build credit profiles from their earnings data.
  • Expense management for Indian SMBs — most Indian companies still use Excel for expense tracking. A WhatsApp-first expense tool would work.
  • GST filing assistant for freelancers — GST compliance is painful for solopreneurs. An affordable, simple tool for the 20 million Indian freelancers.
  • Insurance for gig economy workers — health and accident insurance packaged specifically for delivery partners.
  • Revenue-based financing for D2C brands — Indian D2C brands need working capital but can't get bank loans. Finance against their revenue.

Agritech

  • Direct farmer-to-consumer produce delivery — cut out the 4-5 middlemen between a farmer and your dinner table.
  • Crop insurance using satellite data — satellite imagery can automate claims for the millions of uninsured Indian farmers.
  • WhatsApp-based market price alerts for farmers — farmers sell at mandi prices without knowing if better prices are available nearby.
  • Soil testing on demand — soil health determines crop yield but testing is inaccessible to most Indian farmers.
  • Agri equipment rental marketplace — tractors cost ₹20-30 lakh. A rental marketplace makes them accessible to small farmers.

Healthtech

  • Mental health app in Hindi and regional languages — most mental health apps are English-only. 90% of India is not.
  • Affordable diagnostics booking — blood tests and scans are expensive in cities and unavailable in tier 2 towns.
  • Medicine subscription for chronic conditions — diabetics and hypertension patients need monthly medicines. Auto-delivery with reminder.
  • Home physiotherapy platform — post-surgery physiotherapy is expensive. Connect qualified physios with patients at home.
  • Ayurveda and modern medicine integration app — millions of Indians use both. No app tracks them together.

B2B SaaS for Indian SMBs

  • Inventory management for kirana stores — 12 million kirana stores in India, most tracking stock on paper.
  • WhatsApp order management for local businesses — restaurants and boutiques take orders on WhatsApp but manage them in chaos.
  • Payroll software for businesses under 50 employees — existing payroll software is too complex and expensive for small businesses.
  • Hindi-first CRM for Indian sales teams — most CRM tools are English-only and feel foreign to Indian sales reps.
  • Digital menu and ordering for dhaba chains — small restaurant chains need affordable POS and menu management.
  • Vendor management for manufacturing MSMEs — India has 6 crore MSMEs, most managing suppliers over phone calls.

Vernacular AI and language tech

  • AI customer support in Indian languages — most Indian businesses serve customers who prefer Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, or Bengali.
  • Voice-first banking for rural India — rural Indians have phones but not smartphones. Voice interfaces in local languages.
  • Regional language content creation tools — content creators making YouTube videos in Tamil or Marathi have zero AI tools.
  • Vernacular legal document generator — rent agreements and basic contracts in regional languages for people who can't read English.

Edtech

  • Skill certification for blue-collar workers — electricians, plumbers, and carpenters have no credential system. Build one.
  • Affordable coding bootcamp for tier 2 and 3 cities — most good coding education is in metros. The demand is everywhere.
  • Parent-teacher communication app for government schools — government school teachers communicate with parents via handwritten notes in 2026.
  • Competitive exam prep for UPSC in regional languages — most UPSC content is in English. 80% of aspirants want it in Hindi or their state language.

Climate and sustainability

  • EV charging for apartment complexes — most apartment buildings have no EV charging. Retrofit solutions with shared billing.
  • Carbon credits for Indian farmers — farmers who adopt sustainable practices can earn carbon credits. No one is making this easy in India.
  • Solar panel maintenance marketplace — India has millions of solar rooftop installations. Maintenance is fragmented and unreliable.
  • Plastic waste collection and recycling logistics — connect waste generators with recyclers. The supply chain for plastic recycling in India is broken.

Logistics and supply chain

  • Last-mile delivery for tier 3 and rural India — e-commerce penetration in rural India is growing but last-mile delivery is slow.
  • Cold chain logistics for perishables — India loses 30% of fresh produce due to poor cold chain infrastructure.
  • Trucking marketplace for inter-city freight — most trucking in India is still booked over phone calls.
  • Cross-border shipping simplified for Indian D2C — Indian D2C brands want to sell globally but international shipping is a nightmare.

Consumer and D2C

  • Affordable period care subscription — period poverty affects millions of Indian women. Affordable, subscription-based menstrual hygiene products.
  • Hyperlocal home services in tier 2 cities — Urban Company works in metros. The same model is needed in Nashik, Surat, Coimbatore.
  • Pet care services for India's growing pet economy — India's pet industry is growing 20% per year. Vet, grooming, and food delivery.
  • Wedding vendor marketplace for non-metro cities — most Indian weddings happen outside metros but wedding platforms are metro-focused.

Infrastructure and developer tools

  • Compliance automation for Indian startups — MCA filings, ROC compliance, TDS, and GST automation in one dashboard.
  • API for Indian government data — Aadhaar, PAN, GST, and driving licence verification wrapped in a simple developer API.
  • ONDC-native commerce tools — ONDC is growing. Build seller tools, buyer apps, and logistics integrations on top of it.
  • India-focused cloud optimised for latency — AWS and GCP are global. An India-optimised cloud with data residency for regulated industries.

How to pick your idea

Don't pick the idea that sounds biggest. Pick the problem you personally understand best. The Indian founder advantage is proximity to the problem — you live here, you know what's broken, and you speak the languages your customers speak.

The fastest way to validate any idea: talk to 10 people who have the problem. Don't show them a product. Ask how they currently solve it. If 7 out of 10 are genuinely frustrated and willing to pay, you have a real idea.
