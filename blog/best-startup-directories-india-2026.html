Best startup directories in India 2026

September 2026·7 min read

Getting your startup listed in the right directories is one of the highest-ROI growth moves for an early-stage Indian founder. You get a backlink, discovery from people actively looking for tools, and a permanent digital presence — all for a fraction of what you'd spend on ads.

Here's a breakdown of the best startup directories available to Indian founders in 2026, what each offers, and who each one is best for.

1. StartupOnX India — Best for Indian founders building in public

StartupOnX India (startuponx.com) is India's dedicated startup directory for founders building in public. It's the only directory specifically built for the Indian founder community, with pricing in Indian rupees and UPI payment support.

  • Price: ₹149/month (founding member), ₹299/month (standard)
  • Audience: Indian founders, investors, early adopters
  • Best for: Any Indian startup at any stage
  • Backlink value: Growing domain authority, India-specific SEO value
  • Payment: UPI — no credit card needed

The advantage of StartupOnX India over global directories is the India-specific audience and the UPI payment flow — no international card needed, no currency conversion headache.

2. Product Hunt — Best for global launch day traffic

Product Hunt is the world's largest startup launch platform. A successful launch can drive thousands of visitors in a single day. However, the audience is predominantly US-based, and the competition for visibility is intense.

  • Price: Free to list
  • Audience: Global, primarily US tech audience
  • Best for: B2B SaaS, developer tools, productivity apps
  • Backlink value: High DA, strong SEO benefit
  • Tip: Launch on a Tuesday or Wednesday for best visibility

3. IndieHackers — Best for community and revenue sharing

IndieHackers is a community platform where bootstrapped founders share their revenue numbers, growth stories, and product updates. Listing here builds credibility with other founders and potential early customers.

  • Price: Free
  • Audience: Bootstrapped founders, indie hackers globally
  • Best for: SaaS products with transparent revenue sharing
  • Backlink value: High DA, strong community signals

4. BetaList — Best for pre-launch waitlist building

BetaList helps early-stage startups build a waitlist before they launch. If you're still building and want to generate interest, BetaList is worth submitting to early.

  • Price: Free (paid option for faster listing)
  • Audience: Early adopters, tech enthusiasts
  • Best for: Pre-launch products looking for beta users

5. Crunchbase — Best for investor discovery

Crunchbase is where investors go to research companies. A free Crunchbase profile gives your startup credibility and makes you discoverable to VCs, angels, and acquirers searching for Indian startups.

  • Price: Free for basic profile
  • Audience: Investors, journalists, researchers
  • Best for: Startups seeking funding or press coverage
  • Backlink value: Very high DA — one of the best free backlinks available

6. G2 and Capterra — Best for B2B SaaS reviews

If you have a B2B product, getting listed on G2 and Capterra helps you capture buyers who are actively comparing software solutions. These platforms have very high commercial intent traffic.

  • Price: Free to list, paid options for promotion
  • Audience: Business buyers actively looking for software
  • Best for: B2B SaaS with at least a few customers who can leave reviews

Which directories should you start with?

As an Indian founder, the highest ROI sequence is:

  • Start with StartupOnX India for India-specific visibility
  • Add Crunchbase immediately for the high-DA backlink
  • Submit to IndieHackers to build community credibility
  • Plan a Product Hunt launch when you're ready for a big day
  • Add G2/Capterra once you have a few customers to review you

Each of these is free or low cost. Together they give you a strong backlink profile, multi-channel discovery, and the foundation for SEO growth.
