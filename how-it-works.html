How it works

Getting listed takes less than 5 minutes. Here's the full process.

1
Fill in your startup details

Submit your startup name, a one-line description, category, website URL, and your logo. Keep your description plain and specific — "Gym app for QR check-ins" beats "Revolutionising fitness."

2
Pay ₹149/month via UPI

Scan the UPI QR code on the submit page with GPay, PhonePe, Paytm or any UPI app. Pay exactly ₹149. Then copy your UPI transaction ID and paste it in the form. No credit card needed.

3
We verify and review

We manually verify your UPI payment and review your submission within 24 hours. We check that your startup is real and the description is accurate.

4
Your listing goes live

Once approved, your startup appears on the Discover page. You'll get a confirmation email. Your listing stays live as long as you pay monthly.

Questions

Who can submit a startup?

Any Indian founder or team building a real product. It doesn't need to be funded, profitable, or even launched — pre-launch startups are welcome.

What startups get rejected?

We reject listings that are not real businesses, have misleading descriptions, or violate basic content standards. We don't reject based on stage or revenue.

Can I update my listing after it goes live?

Yes. Email us at hello@startuponx.com with your updated details and we'll update it within 48 hours.

How do I cancel?

Email us at hello@startuponx.com and we'll remove your listing and stop the subscription immediately. No questions asked.

Does my price stay locked at ₹149/month?

Yes. If you're one of the first 25 founders, your price is locked at ₹149/month forever — even when the standard price goes up to ₹299/month.

What do I get from being listed?

Visibility to founders, users, and investors browsing the directory. A backlink to your website. A place in the Indian founder community. And a growing audience as more founders join.
