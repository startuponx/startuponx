About StartupOnX India

startuponx.com is a curated directory of startups Indian founders are building. Pay ₹149/mo to get listed. No ranking by money. No algorithm.

What we are

StartupOnX India is India's startup directory for founders building in public. We list verified Indian startups across categories including AI, SaaS, Fintech, Developer Tools, Design, Marketing, and Productivity. Every listing is manually reviewed before going live.

Why we built this

Indian founders build incredible products that don't get the visibility they deserve. Most startup directories are expensive, US-focused, or algorithm-driven. We built StartupOnX India to be simple, affordable, and honest — ₹149/month, no ranking by money, no algorithm, just Indian startups in a clean directory.

Who it's for

Indian founders at any stage — pre-launch, early revenue, or scaling. If you're building something and want to be discovered by users, investors, and other founders, this is for you.

How it works

Sign in with Google, submit your startup details and logo, pay ₹149/month via UPI, and your listing goes live within 24 hours after review. The first 25 founders lock in ₹149/month forever. After that, the price goes to ₹299/month.
